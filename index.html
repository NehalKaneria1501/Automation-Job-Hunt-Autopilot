Incident Root Cause & Timeline

At 04:12 UTC, Envoy edge ingress proxies stopped terminating TLS traffic for ~4.2% of API requests due to a stale in-memory SSL context. While Kubernetes Secrets were updated by cert-manager, Envoy failed to trigger hot-reloads via inotify.

1. Replaced file-based inotify watches with Envoy SDS (Secret Discovery Service) gRPC streaming.
